# Shamba Guard: User Flows & Ecosystem

This document outlines how different users interact with the Shamba Guard ecosystem, focusing on the relationship between Individual Farmers and Organizations (NGOs, Cooperatives, Corporate Carbon Buyers).

## 1. The Individual Farmer Flow

An individual farmer who owns a smartphone can interact directly with the Shamba Guard platform without needing an intermediary.

### Journey
1. **Self-Onboarding**: The farmer downloads the app, verifies their phone number, and draws their farm boundary on the map.
2. **Policy Purchase**: The farmer selects an insurance tier and pays the premium via M-Pesa.
3. **Direct Protection**: Shamba Guard's satellite monitoring activates. If a drought triggers, the farmer receives an M-Pesa payout directly.
4. **Carbon Earnings**: If the farmer maintains their trees for 12 months, the platform mints a carbon credit. When sold on the global market, the farmer receives the proceeds directly via M-Pesa.

## 2. The Organization Flow (NGOs & Cooperatives)

Organizations use Shamba Guard to protect and manage their specific network of farmers.

### Journey
1. **Agent Registration**: The organization's field workers download the app and register as **Agents**.
2. **Platform Approval**: Shamba Guard Admins verify the organization and approve the Agents.
3. **Assisted Onboarding**: The Agents visit farmers who lack smartphones (basic phone users). Using the Agent App, they register the farmer, draw the farm boundary, and log the agricultural practices.
4. **Group Protection**: The organization can choose to subsidize or pay the insurance premiums on behalf of their farmers.
5. **Impact Monitoring**: The organization uses a web dashboard (Post-MVP) to monitor the drought risk, NDVI health, and carbon capture of their entire farmer network.

## 3. The Corporate Carbon Buyer Flow (Post-MVP)

Corporate entities looking to offset their carbon footprint typically do not use the mobile app. Instead, they interact with the platform via the **Shamba Guard Web Portal** or the Blockchain directly.

### Journey
1. **Browse Impact**: The corporation logs into the web portal and views a dashboard of available, satellite-verified carbon credits.
2. **Purchase & Retire**: The corporation purchases credits. The blockchain permanently "retires" the credit so it cannot be resold.
3. **Direct Payout**: The money paid by the corporation bypasses traditional middlemen and is routed directly to the farmers' M-Pesa accounts via Daraja B2C API.

## 💡 Feature Exploration: The "Join Organization" Marketplace

*This section addresses the product question: What if an individual farmer wants to join an organization later?*

### Is it a good idea?
**Yes, it is a brilliant idea.** In agricultural technology, this is known as "Digital Cooperatives" or "Outgrower Networks".

A farmer might start independently, but later realize that joining a verified organization (like a local Coffee Cooperative or an NGO like One Acre Fund) gives them benefits. For example, the organization might pay their insurance premium for them, or bundle their carbon credits to sell at a higher bulk price.

### Proposed Implementation (Post-MVP Expansion)
We could introduce an **"Explore Orgs"** or **"My Cooperative"** screen within the Farmer App.

**The Future Flow:**
1. **Browse**: The farmer navigates to the "Organizations" screen.
2. **Discover**: They see a list of verified organizations operating in their specific region (e.g., *Meru Dairy Co-op*, *Green Africa Foundation*).
3. **Request**: The farmer taps **"Request to Join"**.
4. **Approval**: The organization receives the request on their admin dashboard and accepts the farmer.
5. **Benefits Unlock**: Once linked, the organization can subsidize the farmer's insurance premiums, or the farmer can receive specialized SMS crop advice generated by the organization's agronomists.
