# Logging and Monitoring Standards

## 1. Purpose

Define logging and monitoring requirements.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all systems and services.

## 3. Standard Requirements

### Security Logging

- Authentication events shall be logged.
- Authorization failures shall be logged.
- Critical system actions shall be recorded.

### Monitoring

- Critical systems shall be continuously monitored.
- Alerts shall be configured for suspicious activity.

### Log Protection

- Logs shall be protected from unauthorized modification or deletion.

### Alerting

- Security alerts shall be generated for anomalies and breaches.

## 4. Verification

- Audit reviews
- Log integrity checks

## 5. Exceptions

Require Security Team approval.

## 6. Review and Maintenance

Reviewed annually.
