# Data Protection Policy

## Purpose
To protect sensitive user, agricultural, environmental, and blockchain-related data.

## Scope
Applies to all data processed by ShambaGuard.

## Data Classification

### Public Data
Information intended for public viewing.

### Internal Data
Operational information restricted to authorized personnel.

### Confidential Data
Personal information, authentication credentials, and system secrets.

## Data Protection Requirements

- Sensitive data shall be encrypted.
- Access shall follow the principle of least privilege.
- Data shall only be collected when necessary.
- Data retention periods shall be defined and enforced.

## Data Disposal

Data shall be securely deleted when no longer required.
